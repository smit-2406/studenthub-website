Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common support questions

On the Login page, click the "Forgot Password?" link below the sign-in form. Enter your registered CHARUSAT email address and you will receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If the email does not arrive, please check your spam folder or contact Technical Support using the form above.

Log in to your StudentHub account and navigate to the Course Catalog. Find your desired course and click the "Enroll" button. You will be directed to the registration confirmation page. Note that enrollment is only available during the official registration window for each semester. Contact Academic Queries support if you experience eligibility issues.

First, try performing a hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac) to clear your browser cache. Ensure you are using an up-to-date version of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. If the issue persists, try opening the portal in an incognito/private window. For ongoing problems, please submit a Technical Issue ticket via the contact form above and include your browser version and a description of the error.

Our support team aims to respond to all inquiries within one business day during office hours (Monday – Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM). Technical issues marked as urgent are typically addressed within a few hours. During peak periods such as semester registration or exam season, response times may be slightly longer. You will receive a reply to your registered email address.
