Notification Preferences

Choose which notifications you'd like to receive.

  • Email Notifications Receive important updates and announcements via email.
  • SMS Alerts Get critical alerts sent directly to your registered mobile number.
  • Assignment Reminders Receive reminders 24 hours before assignment deadlines.
  • Event Notifications Stay informed about campus events, workshops, and seminars.
  • Newsletter Subscribe to the CHARUSAT monthly student newsletter.

Change Password

Use at least 8 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

Data Privacy

Control how your data is used on the StudentHub platform.

  • Public Profile Visibility Allow other students to view your profile information.
  • Share Academic Records Allow authorised faculty to view your grades and attendance.
  • Usage Analytics Help us improve the portal by sharing anonymous usage data.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Add an extra layer of security to your account login.
