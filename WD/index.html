Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries about StudentHub.

StudentHub is the official central digital campus portal of CHARUSAT University. It brings together course management, academic notices, student progress tracking, department announcements, and administrative tools — all in one accessible, responsive web application. Whether you are a student, faculty member, or administrator, StudentHub is designed to simplify your campus experience.

Registering for a new semester is quick and easy. Simply click the Register Now link in the notification banner or navigate to register.html. Fill in your student ID, personal details, and select your desired courses for the upcoming semester. Ensure you complete registration before the stated deadline to avoid late fees. If you face any issues, contact the Help Center.

If you have forgotten your password, go to the Login page and click the "Forgot Password?" link. Enter your registered university email address and you will receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you do not receive the email, please check your spam folder or contact the Help Center for further assistance.
