About StudentHub

Learn about our mission, team, and the university we serve

Our Mission

Why StudentHub exists and what drives us forward every day.

🎯

Empowering Every Student's Academic Journey

StudentHub was created to bridge the gap between students, faculty, and university administration at CHARUSAT University. Our mission is to provide a seamless, accessible, and intelligent digital campus experience that empowers students to manage their academic life efficiently — from course enrollment and progress tracking to receiving real-time notices and connecting with peers.

We believe that technology should serve education, not complicate it. By centralising academic tools into one responsive web portal, we reduce friction and let students focus on what truly matters: learning, growing, and achieving their potential. StudentHub is built with accessibility, inclusivity, and innovation at its core.

About CHARUSAT University

A legacy of academic excellence rooted in Gujarat, India.

🏛️

Our History

Established in 1955, CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) has grown from a small technical institute into one of Gujarat's premier universities. Over seven decades, it has nurtured thousands of engineers, scientists, and business leaders who have made a significant impact nationally and globally.

📍

Location

CHARUSAT University is nestled in the lush campus of Changa village, Anand district, Gujarat. Situated off National Highway 8, the campus is easily accessible from Anand and Nadiad. Spread across a sprawling green campus, it offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, residences, and recreational facilities for students.

Off NH-8, Changa — 388421,
Anand, Gujarat, India
🏆

Accreditation

CHARUSAT is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) and recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Several of its engineering programmes hold NBA accreditation. The university is consistently ranked among the top private technical universities in Western India, reflecting its commitment to quality education and research.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make at StudentHub.

♿

Accessibility

We are committed to building an inclusive portal that every student can use, regardless of ability or device. StudentHub follows WCAG 2.1 guidelines, ensuring screen-reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, high-contrast modes, and responsive layouts for all screen sizes.

💡

Innovation

Technology evolves rapidly, and so does StudentHub. We continuously explore modern web technologies, UX research findings, and student feedback to deliver cutting-edge features — from real-time notifications to personalised dashboards and intelligent course recommendations.

🌟

Excellence

At CHARUSAT, the pursuit of excellence is not just a motto — it is a way of life. StudentHub reflects this ethos by delivering a reliable, performant, and beautifully designed platform that meets the highest standards of software quality, security, and user experience.
